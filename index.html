How it works

Three local steps from raw logs or CSVs to share-safe output.

Step 01

Paste or upload

Paste logs, tickets, or notes, or drop a .txt or .csv file (up to 5 MB) decoded locally via FileReader.

Step 02

Choose a mode

Pick Full Mask, Partial Mask, or Consistent Pseudonymization and toggle individual PII detectors live.

Step 03

Review and download

Audit highlights side-by-side with entity counts, then copy or download the sanitized .txt or .csv file.

What it detects

Each detector pairs linear-time patterns with structural or checksum validation to cut false positives.

Credit Card

Validated with the Luhn (mod 10) checksum and IIN issuer hints so random 16-digit IDs are ignored.

Aadhaar

12-digit UIDAI format validated with the Verhoeff dihedral checksum to catch typos and lookalikes.

Indian PAN

Enforces word boundaries and validates the 4th-character holder code against official entity types.

Phone

Matches Indian (+91 / 0) and E.164 international numbers while rejecting substrings of longer digit runs.

IP Address

Strict 0–255 IPv4 octets without leading zeros, plus full and compressed IPv6 addresses.

Email

Case-insensitive RFC local parts with + tags and subdomains, safe against ReDoS backtracking.

Three redaction modes

Switch strategies on the fly without re-uploading or losing formatting.

Full Mask

Replaces every match with a fixed block or optional typed tag.

[REDACTED] / [REDACTED:EMAIL]

Partial Mask

Keeps minimal verification context while masking sensitive digits.

XXXX-XXXX-0366 · j***@example.test

Pseudonymize

Maps recurring normalized entities to consistent numbered tokens.

USER_01 · PHONE_01 · CARD_01
